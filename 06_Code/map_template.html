Sources, definitions and analytical scope

The map includes the complete-case analytical sample. Eligibility: at least 500 ACS residents, positive land area, available primary outcome and contextual covariates. Excluded tracts are omitted rather than colored as zero. Cafe counts refer to amenity=cafe, after the documented conservative cross-type duplicate rule. Polygon features use their bounding-box midpoint; the manuscript reports sensitivity analyses.

Population and socioeconomic measures: ACS 2018–2022 through CDC/ATSDR SVI 2022. Health: CDC PLACES 2024. Cafe source: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL, obtained through HeiGIT ohsome. Boundaries: US Census Bureau. Full TIGER/Line boundaries were used for analysis; cartographic boundaries simplified to 80 m are used only for display.

Health tooltip intervals are the published CDC 95% intervals for the tract estimate. They are different from the regression intervals in the manuscript. Color scales use pooled study-wide limits, so changing counties does not change the scale. Values beyond the indicated cap use the end color; tooltips retain the underlying values.

Map lines delineate study areas and do not necessarily depict accepted national boundaries. This supplement works offline. D3 v7.9.0 is included under its ISC license. No personal health data, tracking, external map tiles or remote data requests are included.
