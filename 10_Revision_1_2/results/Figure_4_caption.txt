Figure 4. Mapped cafe classification under tract and centroid proximity rules. Panel A uses all 23,527 study tracts; panel B uses the 17,331 tracts with no mapped cafe within their own boundary. Bars exhaustively classify each indicated sample into at least one mapped cafe or none under each rule. Centroid proximity counts use EPSG:5070 planar distances of 500 m or 1 km from the frozen full-resolution geometric tract centroid. The same December 2022 nonduplicate OSM inventory is used, including records in excluded or adjacent tracts. These counts describe geographical measurement and do not measure walking access, residential exposure, cafe attendance, or a health intervention. Source: © OpenStreetMap contributors, 31 December 2022 snapshot; US Census Bureau tract geography. The 500 m and 1 km calculations are exploratory post-results analyses.
